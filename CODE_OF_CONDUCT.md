# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Welcome different backgrounds and skill levels. Respect privacy and copyright.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or block accounts based on the behavior and its impact. Apply these expectations consistently and explain moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

Use GitHub's Report content feature or contact the maintainer through the public GitHub profile to report conduct concerns. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Reports will be handled with reasonable discretion; no absolute confidentiality or response-time guarantee is made. For software vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md.
