# Covers and book titles

The cover library contains 100 designs: 20 original motifs × five palettes. Motifs include flowers, fern, rose, lotus, leaves, butterfly, fox, owl, whale, cat, mountains, waves, moon, constellation, orbit, circuits, geometry, arches, sunrise, and meadow. They are drawn locally from `CoverArt.swift`; no stock-image service, AI API, account, or external image download is involved.

Automatic selection uses a few title keywords and a stable hash. It is a design suggestion, not an understanding of the book's content. The cover picker can override it. Titles use macOS fonts rendered into the cover image; no font files are bundled. Font fallback supports additional scripts. Covers are 1200 × 1800 JPEGs, with high-contrast text and illustrations that remain usable on monochrome readers.

The original decorative artwork generated by the built-in templates is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**, allowing use and sharing of the generated covers. See `licenses/CC0-1.0.txt`. The application source code remains AGPL-3.0-only. This dedication does not apply to book titles, user-supplied images, existing book covers, system fonts, or other third-party content, and grants no trademark or publicity rights. Use only custom images you have permission to use.

Filename cleanup replaces underscores and filename hyphens, normalizes spaces, and preserves short acronyms. The title remains editable. Documents get covers automatically; existing EPUBs and common ebook formats keep their original cover by default. EPUBs left unchanged remain byte-for-byte copies. Explicit title edits or replacement covers modify the EPUB's package metadata; original chapter bytes are retained. A replacement cover is inserted first in the reading order and labelled using EPUB 2/3 cover metadata. Recognized former cover pages are removed from the reading order, while their assets remain in the archive to preserve links. Signed/encrypted EPUBs cannot be customized.

Custom images are fitted proportionally below the title without cropping, and never modify the original image. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, TIFF, up to 40 MB and 40 megapixels. Kindle cover thumbnails and placement still depend on Amazon and the reading device; the app cannot force Amazon to display a particular thumbnail.
